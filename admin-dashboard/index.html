Auth Background Video

Video played as background on Login and Sign up screens. MP4 only. Recommended: 9:16 aspect ratio (e.g. 1080×1920) for mobile, under 15MB. Video is scaled to cover the screen (BoxFit.cover).

Steps: 1) Choose MP4 → Upload to S3 (if you see an error, S3 may not be configured – see backend S3_SETUP.md). 2) The URL will appear below; then click Save. 3) Fully restart the app (or reopen login/signup) to see the video.

Home Hero Graphic

Transparent graphic overlay on the red header (Hello! / What service do you need today?). Use Lottie (.json) or GIF for transparency; the red gradient stays as background. Upload to S3 then Save.

App Branding

App logo (transparent PNG) and welcome text used on Splash, Welcome to ProMech, Login, Sign up, and Mechanic screens. Optional splash animation (Lottie/GIF/MP4) plays when the app opens.

Steps: 1) Choose logo image → Upload to S3 (if you see an error, S3 may not be configured). 2) The URL will appear; then click Save. 3) Fully restart the app to see the logo.
